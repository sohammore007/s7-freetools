How to Use This BMI Calculator

Body Mass Index (BMI) is a standard mathematical screening tool used globally to categorize a person's weight relative to their height. While it does not directly measure body fat or account for muscle mass density, it provides a very reliable generalized indicator of healthy weight ranges for adult men and women.

Using our completely free calculator is simple: select your preferred measurement system (Metric or Imperial), enter your current height and weight, and click calculate. The tool immediately crunches the math within your own browser without sending your personal health metrics to any external servers, ensuring 100% privacy.

The visual scale will plot your score according to the official World Health Organization (WHO) BMI classifications: Underweight (Under 18.5), Normal Weight (18.5 – 24.9), Overweight (25 – 29.9), and Obese (30 and above).

Frequently Asked Questions

Is BMI accurate for everyone?

No. Because BMI only relies on height and weight, it cannot distinguish between muscle weight and fat. Highly muscular athletes may register as "Overweight" or "Obese" despite having very low body fat.

Are my height and weight saved?

No. This tool operates 100% offline within your web browser. Nothing is tracked, uploaded, or saved anywhere.

Does BMI differ for men and women?

The standard adult mathematical formula and WHO categorizations are identical for both adult men and adult women. However, interpretations by medical professionals may slightly differ based on gender and age.

Can I use this for children?

No, this calculator uses the standard adult formula. Children and teenagers require a specialized BMI percentile chart that factors in their exact age in months and gender.
