How to Use This Age Calculator

Have you ever been asked for your exact age in months and days, or wondered exactly how many days you've been alive? Calculating this manually can be incredibly tedious due to leap years and the varying lengths of each calendar month. Our free online age calculator does all the heavy lifting for you instantly.

To use the tool, simply click on the "Date of Birth" input field and select your birth date from the calendar pop-up (or type it in directly). Once you hit the "Calculate Age" button, the tool will immediately display your exact chronological age in a readable format of years, months, and days.

But the insights don't stop there. Below your exact age, you will also discover the total number of days you have lived since you were born. Additionally, the calculator looks ahead to your upcoming birthday and tells you exactly how many days are left until you can celebrate again! Best of all, because this tool is built entirely with client-side JavaScript, all calculations are securely performed directly on your device. Your sensitive birth date information is never uploaded, tracked, or saved on our servers.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the age calculator calculate my age?

The calculator compares your chosen date of birth against today's date. It calculates the difference in years, months, and days accounting for leap years and the varying lengths of months.

Is my date of birth saved anywhere?

No, your date of birth is completely private. All calculations are performed instantly using JavaScript entirely within your web browser, meaning your data never leaves your device.

How do you calculate the days until my next birthday?

The tool determines your birthday for the current year. If that date has already passed, it calculates the number of days between today and your birthday in the following year.

What time zone is used?

The calculator uses the local date and time on your device to determine "today", ensuring the result is accurate for your specific location.
