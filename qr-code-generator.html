How to Generate a QR Code Online

Welcome to our completely free, offline-capable QR code generator. QR codes (Quick Response codes) have become an essential tool for linking physical items to digital destinations. Whether you are creating a link to a restaurant menu, sharing Wi-Fi credentials, or directing users to your new website, this tool makes it incredibly easy.

To use this tool, simply paste your target URL or any raw text into the input box and click "Generate QR Code". Your code is instantly mathematically generated using your browser's processing power. This means we never send your destination link or text to an external server—guaranteeing complete privacy and speed.

Once your code is visible, you can safely test it by pointing your smartphone camera directly at your computer monitor. When you're ready, click "Download PNG" to save a high-quality, perfectly scannable image file to your device. Our QR codes never expire because they contain the raw data directly, rather than relying on shady redirect links.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do these QR codes ever expire?

No! Because this tool generates "static" QR codes that contain your exact text or URL embedded within the pixels, they will work forever. They do not rely on redirect services.

Is my data sent to a server?

Absolutely not. Our QR generator is powered entirely by vanilla JavaScript running locally in your browser. Nothing is uploaded, keeping your data entirely private.

Can I use this for commercial purposes?

Yes. You can download and print the generated PNG files on business cards, posters, menus, or product packaging without any licensing restrictions.

How do I test my QR code?

Simply open the default Camera app on any modern iOS or Android device and point it at the generated code on your screen. A link bubble should appear instantly.
