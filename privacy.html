Privacy Policy

We believe your data belongs to you. Here is exactly how we handle it.

At S7 FreeTools, your privacy is our top priority. Because of how we have architected our tools, we naturally collect as little information as possible. This policy outlines how we handle your data.

1. Client-Side Tool Processing

The core philosophy of S7 FreeTools is that your data belongs to you. All of our utilities—including the image compressor, word counter, and age calculator—are powered by client-side JavaScript. This means that when you process an image or text, the computation happens entirely on your own device (within your browser). Your files and input data are never uploaded to our servers.

2. No Account or Personal Data Collected

We do not require you to create an account to use any of our tools. We do not ask for your name, email address, or any other personally identifiable information. Because we don't ask for it, we don't store it, and we certainly don't sell it.

3. Cookies and Local Storage

Our website itself does not use cookies to track your personal identity. We may use anonymous browser LocalStorage strictly for functional purposes (for example, remembering a dark mode preference if implemented), but this data never leaves your device.

4. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdSense)

To keep S7 FreeTools free to use, we may display advertisements provided by Google AdSense. Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to our website or other websites on the internet. These cookies enable Google to serve personalized ads. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ad Settings page.
