How to Create the Perfect Color Palette

Choosing the right colors for a website, illustration, or brand identity can be challenging. A completely random set of RGB values usually looks chaotic and jarring. To create harmony, designers rely on color theory.

Our Color Palette Generator doesn't just pick random colors. It selects a random base hue and then uses established mathematical formulas (like Analogous, Complementary, and Triadic spacing on the color wheel) to generate the remaining colors. This ensures that no matter how many times you click generate, the resulting palette will always look cohesive and professional.

Tips for usage: If you see a color you love, click the lock icon. When you generate again, the generator will build the new palette around your locked selections.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the color palette generator work?

Unlike simple random generators that often create clashing colors, our generator uses established color theory. It picks a random base hue and then derives complementary, analogous, and triadic colors using HSL (Hue, Saturation, Lightness) math to ensure the palette is always cohesive.

Can I keep a color I like and change the others?

Yes! Just click the 'Lock' icon on any color swatch. When you click 'Generate Palette' again, the locked colors will remain exactly the same while the unlocked ones will be regenerated to match.
