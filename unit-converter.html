How to Use This Unit Converter

Whether you're following a recipe from another country, working on a DIY engineering project, or checking the weather abroad, accurate unit conversion is a must-have tool. Our S7 FreeTools Unit Converter makes this math instant and effortless.

To use the tool, first select your desired category from the drop-down menu (Length, Weight, or Temperature). The unit options will automatically update to match your category. Next, choose your "From" unit and your "To" unit, and type the number you want to convert. The calculation is done dynamically in real-time as you type, using pure browser-based math for zero latency.

Because all conversions are calculated client-side, this tool works entirely offline without needing an API or external service. You can rely on it to be 100% private, blazingly fast, and completely free of charge.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which units are supported?

Currently, we support common Length units (cm, m, km, inches, feet, miles), Weight units (kg, g, lbs, oz), and Temperature scales (Celsius, Fahrenheit, Kelvin).

Do I need an internet connection to convert?

Once the page loads, no internet connection is required. The math is calculated directly on your device.

Are the conversions perfectly accurate?

Yes, we use standardized mathematical constants for all conversions, ensuring precision down to multiple decimal places.

Can I use negative numbers?

Yes, the tool fully supports negative numbers, which is especially useful when converting sub-zero temperatures.
