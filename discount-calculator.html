How to Calculate a Discount

Whether you're shopping in a store or trying to figure out if an online sale is actually a good deal, knowing how to calculate discounts is a valuable skill. Our Discount Calculator does the heavy lifting for you instantly with any currency.

Standard Mode: Find Final Price
If a jacket originally costs 1000 and is 20% off, the calculator finds the discount amount by multiplying 1000 × (20/100) = 200. It then subtracts that 200 from the 1000 to give you a final price of 800.

Reverse Mode: Find Discount Percentage
Sometimes a store just says "Was 1000, Now 800" without telling you the percentage. Toggle to the "Find Discount %" mode, enter those two numbers, and the tool will instantly tell you that you're getting a 20% discount.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do you calculate a discount manually?

To calculate the discount amount, multiply the original price by the discount percentage and divide by 100. Then subtract that amount from the original price to find the final sale price.

How do I find the discount percentage if I know the final price?

Subtract the final price from the original price to get the discount amount. Then divide the discount amount by the original price and multiply by 100 to get the percentage.
